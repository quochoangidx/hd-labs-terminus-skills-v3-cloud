The package in `/app/src/cellbank` turns a cell line's passage log into the lineage report our core files for the line, and QA no longer trusts it. Doublings do not match the viable figures on the count sheets, two flasks seeded from the same harvest come out with different PDLs, a culture grown from a thawed bank vial starts again from the supplier's PDL and one passage too high, a line whose flask crashed near the end of its life drops back out of the limit warnings, and the banks still list vials we have already thawed. The core's procedure, `/app/docs/cell-bank-sop.md` (SOP CB-3), is the authority. Change the package so that the report printed by `python3 /app/tools/cellbank_run.py LOG.json` follows the SOP, and every rule in it holds for every log within the limits of its section 1, not only for logs like the one in `/app/examples/`. Leave `/app/tools/cellbank_run.py` exactly as it is: logs are run through our own copy of that driver against your `/app/src`, and the file you leave there must still match it byte for byte.

Change only what the SOP rules on. Where the SOP has no rule for a figure that one of the package's steps works out, that step keeps working it out as it does today, from the figures the SOP does define, and every other step still follows the SOP; add no exception, clamp or guard of your own. Some inputs are left entirely open and the package may do whatever it likes with them: logs with a figure outside the ranges of section 1, logs that break its structural rules (a `source` or `vial` that does not name an earlier record of the right kind, a freeze thawed more times than it has vials), and fields the README does not describe.

Logs of one to four hundred records over one to four lines will be run: several lineages of one line interleaved in the log, sister cultures from one harvest, supplier vials and bank vials thawed from several banks of a line, freezes taken from any culture, cultures that lost cells, lines reaching and passing their approved maximum, thaws and harvests counted at every viability from 1.0 to 100.0, and banks with none, some or all of their vials thawed. Every log keeps within section 1: cells, seeded and harvested from 1,000 to 10^9, viabilities with one decimal place, one to five hundred vials per freeze, seed PDLs from 0 to 100, seed passages from 0 to 50, approved maxima from 10 to 300, PDLs along every lineage from -100 to 1,000, and no age within 10^-6 of a flag threshold. In the report, every `id`, `line`, `flag` and `bank`, every `passage` and `vials_left` as a JSON integer, and the order of both lists are compared exactly; every other number must agree with the expected one to within one part in 10^9 of its size, or within 10^-9 when the expected number is smaller than one.
