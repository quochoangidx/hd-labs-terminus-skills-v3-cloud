The audit package in `/app/src/hoslog` checks a driver's duty-status log against the fleet's hours-of-service manual, `/app/docs/hours-manual.md` (FM-4), and the safety team has stopped trusting its reports. Drivers who ran past ten hours at the wheel, or drove late into their window, come out clean, violation minutes turn up on the day the shift began rather than the day they were driven, and the hours left at the end of a log are often more generous than what dispatch works out by hand. The manual is the authority wherever the package disagrees with it. Change the package so that the report printed by `python3 /app/tools/hoslog_run.py LOG.json` follows FM-4. Every rule in the manual holds for every log within the limits of its section 1, not only for logs like the one in `/app/examples/`. Leave `/app/tools/hoslog_run.py` exactly as it is: logs are audited by running our own copy of that driver against your `/app/src`, and the file you leave in place must still match it byte for byte.

Change only what the manual governs, and add no correction, clamp or guard that it does not call for. Where the manual gives no rule for a value the package reports, still work out every figure that value is made from as the manual says, and keep only the step the code takes today to turn those figures into the reported value; keeping that step takes precedence over the rest of this paragraph, and every other step still follows the manual. Logs that break section 1 are left entirely open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them: logs longer than fourteen days or with more than 2000 entries, a status the manual does not list, a recap that is not seven days long, and miles past 1000.

Logs of one to fourteen days will be audited: shifts that begin at any minute and run across midnight, resets and breaks made of off-duty time, sleeper-berth time or both, rest periods exactly on the break and reset lengths and one minute short of them, entries of every status with and without miles, driving that reaches each limit exactly and runs past it, several limits passed in the same minutes, recaps from idle weeks to full ones, and logs that end on shift, in rest or past a limit. Every key, every integer and the order of `days` are compared exactly, time left is compared as a number to within 0.001, and every log keeps within section 1 of the manual.
