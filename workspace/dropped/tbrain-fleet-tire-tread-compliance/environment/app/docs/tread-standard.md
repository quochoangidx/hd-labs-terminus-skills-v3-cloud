# Fleet Tire Tread Standard (edition TS-6)

Kestrel Line Haul, Tire Shop.

This standard governs the tire compliance summary the tire shop issues from its
tread inspection log. Where the summary package and this standard disagree,
the standard is right. Section numbers stay the same from one edition to the
next.

## 1. Units, rounding and limits

1.1 Tread depth is kept in tenths of a millimetre and distance in whole
kilometres. When a rule divides a figure, the result is rounded to the nearest
whole unit it is kept in, and an exact half goes up, to the larger number. A
figure that no rule divides is not rounded.

1.2 Dates are calendar dates written YYYY-MM-DD. Every date in a job falls in
the years 2015 to 2034, and none is later than the job's report date.

1.3 A job gives the report date, the shop's tread gauges and the tires to be
summarised. It has 1 to 20 gauges, each with a gauge number of 1 to 12
characters that no other gauge shares and a calibration offset of -9 to 9
tenths, the amount the reference block says must be added to what the gauge
shows. It has 1 to 300 tires.

1.4 Each tire has a tire number of 1 to 16 characters that no other tire in
the job shares; its position, steer, drive or trailer; the tread depth it had
when new, 60 to 320 tenths; the date its casing was made; the number of times
it has been retreaded, 0 to 4; and the odometer, 0 to 5,000,000 km, of the
vehicle it is on at the day it was mounted there, new.

1.5 Each tire has 2 to 30 readings, listed in date order, each giving its
date, the odometer at the reading, the figure the gauge showed, 10 to 320
tenths, and the gauge number, which is one of the job's gauges. The dates are
all different, the odometer rises from each reading to the next, and it is
above the odometer at mounting. The first reading's depth is never above the
tread depth the tire had when new, nor more than 10 tenths below it.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 A tire's first reading and latest reading are the earliest and the latest
of its readings.

2.2 A tire's removal depth is 40 tenths at a steer position and 30 tenths at a
drive or trailer position.

## 3. Depth

3.1 A reading's depth is the figure the gauge showed plus the gauge's
calibration offset.

## 4. Wear

4.1 A tire's latest depth is the depth of its latest reading.

4.2 A tire's tread worn is the tread depth it had when new less its latest
depth, over the distance from the odometer at mounting to the odometer at its
latest reading.

4.3 A tire's wear rate is its tread worn times 10,000 divided by the distance
it was worn over: tenths per 10,000 km.

## 5. Status

5.1 A tire whose latest depth is at or below its removal depth is to be
pulled. A tire that is not to be pulled but whose latest depth is at or below
its removal depth plus 16 tenths is on watch. Any other tire is in service.

## 6. Retread

6.1 A casing's age is the number of days from the date it was made to the
report date. Only a tire that is to be pulled goes for retread, and its casing
may go when its age is below 2,190 days and it has been retreaded fewer than 2
times.

## 7. The summary

7.1 The summary gives one entry per tire, in the order of the job's tires.
