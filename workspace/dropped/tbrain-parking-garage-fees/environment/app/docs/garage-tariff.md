# Garage Tariff GT-2

Quayside Centre car park, operator's office. Second issue.

This tariff sets the parking fees charged at the exit barrier. Where the
charging package and this tariff disagree, the tariff governs. Rule numbers do
not move between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 Times are whole minutes. Money is a whole number of cents.

1.2 This tariff provides for exits files within these limits, and each limit
includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 sessions, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to
  10 characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a vehicle is "CAR", "VAN" or "MOTO";
- an entry minute is from 0 to 100,000, and a session leaves at its entry
  minute or up to 10,080 minutes (seven days) later.

## 2 Stay and hours

2.1 A session's stay is its exit minute less its entry minute. Its charged
hours are every hour of its stay that has begun, so a stay of 61 minutes is
charged 2 hours and a stay of 0 minutes none.

2.2 A car or a van whose stay is 15 minutes or less is in its grace period.

## 3 Parking fee

3.1 The hourly rate is 300 cents for a car, 450 cents for a van and 150 cents
for a motorcycle.

3.2 The parking fee of a session in its grace period is nothing. The parking
fee of any other session is its charged hours at its hourly rate.

3.3 The parking fee of a car or a van is never more than 2,400 cents for each
24 hours of its stay that have begun.
