The stamping line plans its presses with `/app/planner/plan_week.py`, which sends each job to whichever press finishes it soonest in due-date order, and the schedules it makes waste hours on die changes and still deliver late. For each week in `/app/weeks/` (`week-36`, `week-37`, `week-38` and `week-39`), write a schedule to `/app/schedules/<week>.json` that follows `/app/docs/press-rules.md` and costs no more than the week's target in `/app/weeks/targets.json`. Each target is 6 percent above the cost of the best schedule the planning office has found for that week.

`/app/tools/check_schedule.py` runs a schedule under the rules and prints its cost; the rules document gives the file format. Only the four schedule files are used, and how you produce them is up to you, but there is no network and nothing can be installed, so any search you run has to use what is already here (Python 3.13 and its standard library).
