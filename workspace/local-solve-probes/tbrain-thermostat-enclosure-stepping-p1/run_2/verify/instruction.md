`thermostep.simulate` in `/app/src/thermostep` steps our two-node enclosure model (chamber and wall, with an optional on/off thermostat on the chamber) through a sampled schedule, and the numbers it gives are not the ones the model defines. Its design note, `/app/docs/stepping-note.md`, is the authority for the model, the schedule, the thermostat and what a run reports. Change the package so that `simulate` gives what the note says, keeping the public surface as it is: `Network`, `Thermostat`, `Run` and `simulate(network, times, ambient, power, t1, t2, heater_on=True, thermostat=None)`.

Runs will cover networks of every shape the note allows, from coupled nodes to walls with no path to ambient or no coupling at all, schedules of one to a few hundred samples with unequal intervals, ambient swings, heater powers that change between intervals, and thermostats with bands from narrow to wide. Reported temperatures must be within 1e-6 K of the model's, switch times within 1e-6 s, and the heater states and the number of switches must match exactly. No run has `T1` merely graze a set point.
