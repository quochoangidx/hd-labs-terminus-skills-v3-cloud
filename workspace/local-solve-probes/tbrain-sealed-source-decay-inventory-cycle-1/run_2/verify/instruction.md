The Radiation Protection Office's quarterly sealed-source survey, the `sealsrc` package in /app/src/sealsrc, has drifted away from the Office's Source Inventory Manual RPO-7 at /app/docs/source-inventory-manual.md. Hand checks of the last survey turned up current activities that come out low against a calculator, source ages that slip by a day or two around the end of a month, store postings that still carry the certificate figures, and exemption, leak-test and decay-to-disposal calls that disagree with the Office's own records. Please bring the package into line with the manual. Each of the manual's rules is meant for every inventory inside the limits its section 1 sets, and not only for the sort of figures a routine quarter happens to produce. We will produce each survey by putting our own copy of /app/tools/sealsrc_run.py at that path and running `python3 /app/tools/sealsrc_run.py INVENTORY.json` against the sources under /app/src, so leave the driver alone and keep the report layout that /app/README.md describes.

Change only what the manual decides. Where the manual gives no rule for a figure it defines, the answer the package gives for that figure today is the one the Office wants, so add no exception, clamp or guard of your own for an input the manual does not provide for. For such an input the step the package takes today stays, and every other step the input passes through still follows the manual. Some inputs are left entirely open, and nothing is expected of the package for them: figures or dates outside the section 1 limits, a nuclide that Table 2 does not list, an entry naming a location the inventory does not list, a repeated entry or location id, and a file that is not an inventory in the README's layout.

The surveys we run will include inventories where many entries share a store, reference, wipe and survey dates on the last day of a month and on 29 February, reference dates before, on and after the survey date, sources of every nuclide in Table 2 at ages up to far beyond ten half-lives, activities that sit exactly on an exempt quantity, a leak-test threshold or a location limit, entries never wiped and entries with many wipes returned in any order, stores that hold nothing, and the kinds of input named above. Every inventory keeps within the manual's section 1: reference activities from 1 Bq to 1.0e14 Bq, every date from 1950-01-01 to 2099-12-31 with no wipe dated after the survey, removable activities from 0 Bq to 1.0e6 Bq, up to 50 wipes of one entry, location limits from 1 Bq to 1.0e18 Bq, up to 5000 entries and from 1 to 200 locations, unique entry and location ids, and every entry at a location the inventory lists.
