Our cooperative observer network publishes a monthly climatological summary for every station, made from the observers' forms by the package in `/app/src/coopsum`, and the summaries have drifted away from the network handbook, `/app/docs/observer-handbook.md` (CN-7 part 3). Stations that read their instruments in the morning see a warm afternoon and an evening's rain turn up against the following day, and the last evening of a month lands in the next month's summary. Heating and cooling degree days disagree with the figures our utility partners work out day by day, the mean temperature of a well-kept month is off from the national centre's by a tenth or two, days that reached exactly 90.0, 32.0 or 0.0 degrees or exactly a tenth or an inch of rain are missing from the counts, a record that was matched later in the month is shown on its first day, and stations that log many traces are credited hundredths their gauge never caught. The handbook is the authority for the summary. Change the package so that the JSON printed by `python3 /app/tools/coopsum_run.py FORM.json` follows it, and so that each of its rules holds for every form within the limits of its section 1.5, not only for forms that look like `/app/examples/two-stations.json`. The office makes each summary by running its own copy of `/app/tools/coopsum_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave the driver alone: the file at that path must stay byte-for-byte the one you were given.

Correct only what the handbook rules on. Every rule of the handbook applies to exactly the readings, days and months that its own defined terms describe, and to nothing beyond them. Wherever no rule applies to a reading, to what is done with it, or to a figure, the summary keeps what the package does with it today, worked out from the values the handbook defines, and whatever is then worked out from it follows the handbook as usual. Add no exception, clamp or guard of your own. Some forms are left entirely open and the package may do anything with them: values outside the limits of section 1.5, a station whose `days` do not match its month, entries not written the way the README describes, and files that are not forms at all.

Forms of one to forty stations will be run, for months from January 1950 to December 2099 with Februaries of leap and common years among them, and for observation hours anywhere from 0 to 23, midnight and noon included. They hold temperatures anywhere from -60.0 to 130.0, maxima and minima of exactly 90.0, 32.0 and 0.0, days with no maximum, no minimum or no precipitation reading, months in which anything from none to all of the days lack a maximum or a minimum, precipitation amounts from 0.00 to 20.00 inches, traces, amounts of exactly 0.10 and 1.00, gauges left unread for one to six observations in a row, at the end of a month as well as within it, and highest, lowest and wettest days that are matched on a later day. Every value in the summary is compared exactly, its JSON type included, and so is the order of the stations.
