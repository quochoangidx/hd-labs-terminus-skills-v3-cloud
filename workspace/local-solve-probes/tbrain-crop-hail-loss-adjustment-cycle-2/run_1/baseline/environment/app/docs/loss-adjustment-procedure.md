# Crop-Hail Loss Adjustment Procedure (edition 7)

This procedure governs how the claims office turns an adjuster's field
inspection sheets into the payment on a crop-hail claim for field corn. Where
the claims package and this procedure disagree, the procedure is right. Section
numbers stay the same from one edition to the next.

## 1. Units, rounding and limits

1.1 Money is kept in whole cents. Percentages are kept in tenths of a per cent,
so 12.5 per cent is kept as 125. When a rule divides one figure by another or
takes an average, the result is rounded to the nearest cent or the nearest
tenth of a per cent, and an exact half goes up. A figure no rule divides or
averages is not rounded.

1.2 A job holds 1 to 50 claims. Each claim has a claim number of 1 to 16
characters that no other claim in the job shares, and 1 to 30 insured fields.

1.3 Each field has a field code of 1 to 16 characters that no other field of its
claim shares; its insured acres, 1.0 to 5,000.0, kept in tenths of an acre; its
amount of insurance per acre, 10 to 2,000 whole dollars; the deductible option on
the policy (1.6); the growth stage of the crop on the day of the storm, one of
the stages of the leaf-loss chart (4.3); the acres the grower replanted after
the storm, from none to the whole of its insured acres, in tenths of an acre;
and its sample plots.

1.4 A sample plot is a length of row the adjuster counts. A field has 1 to 40
plots. Each gives its stand, the plants in the row before the storm, 20 to 200;
the plants found dead or broken below the ear, from none to the whole stand;
and the leaf area the surviving plants lost, 0 to 1,000 tenths of a per cent.
Adjusters lay plots across the whole field, not only where the storm hit
hardest, so some plots in lightly hit strips show only a plant or two dead or
broken; the sheet records every plot the same way.

1.5 A grower sometimes replants a whole field after a storm, and sometimes only
a washed-out corner or a low spot the storm's rain drowned.

1.6 The deductible options are `full` (no deductible), `straight` and
`vanishing`.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 A hail-thinned plot is a sample plot on which one plant in ten of its
stand, or more, is dead or broken.

2.2 A replanted field is a field on which the grower replanted 10.0 acres or
more.

2.3 A field's liability is its amount of insurance per acre times its insured
acres.

## 3. Sample plots

3.1 The stand loss of a hail-thinned plot is its dead and broken plants
divided by its stand.

3.2 Every sample plot has one stand-loss figure and one leaf-damage figure. A
plot's leaf-damage figure is the leaf area its surviving plants lost.

3.3 A field's stand loss is the average of the stand-loss figures of its plots,
and its defoliation is the average of their leaf-damage figures. Each average is
taken over all of the field's plots.

## 4. Field loss

4.1 A field's leaf loss is its defoliation times the percentage the leaf-loss
chart gives for the field's growth stage, divided by 100 per cent.

4.2 Leaf loss falls only on the plants the storm left standing. A field's loss
is its stand loss plus its leaf loss times what remains once the stand loss is
taken from 100 per cent, divided by 100 per cent.

4.3 The leaf-loss chart:

| Growth stage | Share of defoliation that is lost yield |
|---|---|
| V6 | 5 per cent |
| V10 | 15 per cent |
| V14 | 35 per cent |
| VT | 100 per cent |
| R2 | 75 per cent |
| R4 | 40 per cent |
| R5 | 15 per cent |

## 5. Payable loss and indemnity

5.1 A field whose loss is below 8.0 per cent has a payable loss of nought.

5.2 Any other field's payable loss depends on the deductible option. Under
`full` it is the loss. Under `straight` it is the loss less 10.0 per cent, but
not below nought. Under `vanishing` it is twice the amount by which the loss
exceeds 20.0 per cent, not below nought and never more than the loss.

5.3 A field's indemnity is its liability times its payable loss, divided by
100 per cent.

## 6. Replanting

6.1 A replanted field earns a replant payment of 3,000 cents for each acre
replanted.

6.2 Every field whose sheet records replanted acres has one replant line on
its statement; a replant line may be of any amount, nought included. A field's
payment is its indemnity plus its replant line.

## 7. Claim payment

7.1 A claim's total is the payments of its fields added together.

7.2 A claim whose total is 10,000 cents or more is paid in full. A smaller
claim is not paid.
