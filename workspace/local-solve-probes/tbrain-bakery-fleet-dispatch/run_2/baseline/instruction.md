Dispatch runs the bakery's wholesale deliveries from plans made by `/app/planner/plan_morning.py`, and those plans cost far too much; on two of the next four mornings they do not even reach every customer. Replace them. For each morning in `/app/orders/` (`harbour-lanes`, `old-town`, `riverside` and `north-ring`), write a plan to `/app/plans/<morning>.json` that obeys the dispatch rules in `/app/docs/dispatch-rules.md` and costs no more than that morning's target in `/app/orders/targets.json`. Each target is the most the dispatch office will pay for that morning.

`/app/tools/check_plan.py` applies the rules to a plan and prints its cost, and the rules document gives the plan format. Only the four plan files are used, and how you produce them is up to you, but there is no network and nothing can be installed, so any search you run has to use what is already here (Python 3.13 and its standard library).
