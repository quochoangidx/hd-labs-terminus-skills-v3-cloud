We are retiring the Fortran 77 band model in `/app/legacy/bandmd.f` and moving it to Python. Finish `/app/src/bandmodel/band.py` so that `Band` reproduces the Fortran bit for bit:

- `Band(we0, sm10, sm20)` is one band's program, starting with `CALL BANDIN(WE0, SM10, SM20)`. Several bands run side by side, each as its own program.
- `band.day(px, ta)` is one `CALL BANDDY(PX, TA, NHR, RES, IRES, CODE, *)` with `NHR = len(ta)` and `px` the same length as `ta`. It returns `None` when `BANDDY` takes its alternate return. Otherwise it returns `(RES, IRES, NHR, CODE)` as `BANDDY` leaves them: `RES` a tuple of its 9 REAL values, `IRES` a tuple of its 6 INTEGER values, `NHR` an int and `CODE` a 6-character str.
- After every call, the attributes `we`, `liqw`, `heat`, `kdays` and `bal` hold `COMMON /PACK/`, `sm1` and `sm2` hold `SM1` and `SM2` of `COMMON /SOIL/`, and `qbuf` (a list of 24 values), `npos` and `qtot` hold `COMMON /ROUTE/` as the Fortran leaves them.

The reference is the Fortran compiled by `gfortran -std=legacy -O0 -ffp-contract=off` on x86-64. INTEGER and REAL are 32 bits and DOUBLE PRECISION is 64 bits. Every REAL operation is one IEEE-754 single-precision operation, rounded to nearest with ties to even. Every expression is evaluated as written: parentheses first, then left to right within a precedence level, with any conversion between types made at the operation where the two types meet. The intrinsics follow the Fortran 77 standard. Return each REAL or DOUBLE PRECISION value as a Python float holding exactly that value, and each INTEGER as an int. The inputs `we0`, `sm10`, `sm20` and every element of `px` and `ta` are floats exactly representable in single precision and never negative zero; a day has from 0 to 25 entries. Use only the Python standard library.

Your port will be run against the compiled Fortran on many band runs of up to a few hundred days: snow and rain, warm and cold spells, dry spells, soils filling past both layers, days that stop early at a missing hour (a temperature below -90), days with no hours or too many, and temperatures that fall exactly on rounding and freezing boundaries. Every returned value and every attribute is compared exactly after every call.
