`/app/src/matchbook` is the matching engine for one of our instruments. It handles plain limit and market orders today; iceberg orders, self-trade prevention, fill-or-kill and stop orders are missing. Complete it so that it follows every rule in `/app/docs/matching-rules.md`, which is the authority, keeping its interface as it is: `Engine()`, `Engine.submit(command)` returning the list of events, `Engine.book()` and `Engine.held()`, with the command, event and snapshot shapes the note gives.

The engine will be driven through command sequences of a few to a few thousand commands, with several owners on both sides: icebergs that are hit through several slices in one match, self-trades against ordinary and iceberg orders, fill-or-kill orders that just can and just cannot fill, stop orders that trigger at once, in chains, or not at all, and cancels of resting, held, filled and unknown orders. After every command, its event list, `Engine.book()` and `Engine.held()` are compared exactly with what the note gives.
