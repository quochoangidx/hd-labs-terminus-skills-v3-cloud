Our molecular-biology core facility returns relative-expression reports to its users from the package in `/app/src/qpcrrel`, which reduces a real-time PCR plate export and its plate map, and its numbers have stopped agreeing with what our written procedure gives when a plate is worked by hand. That procedure is `/app/docs/relative-expression-sop.md` (SOP QP-7). Fold changes drift for genes whose dilution series amplify faster than perfect doubling, a standard curve bends when its most dilute level never came up, a sample with two stray wells among five is averaged as if nothing were wrong, and a sample in which a reference gene never came up still gets a fold change. The SOP is the authority for this report: change the package so that `python3 /app/tools/qpcrrel_run.py PLATE.json` prints what the SOP gives. Its rules hold for every plate inside the limits of its section 1, not only for plates shaped like the one in `/app/examples/`. Plates will be reduced by running our own copy of `/app/tools/qpcrrel_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave that driver as it is; the file you leave in its place must match ours byte for byte.

The SOP settles what it has a rule for, and only that is yours to change. Should a plate inside section 1 ever need a value the SOP has no rule for, that value is the one the present package computes for it when its present code is run on the values the SOP settles, and everything worked out from it afterwards still follows the SOP. Add no exception, clamp or guard of your own. A few inputs are left entirely open, and the package may treat them however it likes: Ct values, quantities or counts outside section 1, dilution series whose curve points break the slope limits of section 1, a replicate lying exactly 0.50 cycles from its median, repeated gene names, and files that do not follow `/app/README.md`.

Plates from one to forty-eight samples and up to 384 wells will be reduced, with one to four reference genes and one to eight target genes named anything, and any sample as the calibrator. They include one to six replicates per sample and gene, Cts anywhere from 5.00 to 45.00 and `Undetermined` wells in every role, replicates exactly at the 35.00 cut-off and earlier than 10.00, replicate sets with one or several wells far from the rest, genes with no standards and genes with dilution series of up to eight levels of one to four wells, standard and NTC wells at any Ct the section allows, levels whose wells disagree with one another, standard series with `Undetermined` wells, amplification factors on both sides of 2, and genes that are not detected in a sample or in the calibrator. Wells may come in any order. The `plate`, `gene`, `sample`, `contaminated` and `flags` values, every `null` and the order of both lists are compared exactly. Every `factor`, `mean_ct` and `fold_change` has to be a JSON number; one within one part in 10^9 of the expected value passes, and one further off than one part in 10^8 fails.
