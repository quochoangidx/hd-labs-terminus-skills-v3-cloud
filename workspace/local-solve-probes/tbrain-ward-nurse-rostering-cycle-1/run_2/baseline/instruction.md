The ward office builds its four-week rosters with `/app/planner/plan_ward.py`, which fills each shift with whoever is furthest below their maximum hours, and its rosters cover every shift but ignore the nurses' requests, split their weekends and leave some nurses far off their contracted hours. For each ward in `/app/wards/` (`ash`, `birch`, `cedar` and `dale`), write a roster to `/app/rosters/<ward>.json` that follows `/app/docs/roster-rules.md` and has a penalty no higher than the ward's target in `/app/wards/targets.json`. Each target is the penalty of the best roster the office has found for that ward.

`/app/tools/check_roster.py` checks a roster under the rules and prints its penalty; the rules document gives the file format. Only the four roster files are used, and how you produce them is up to you, but there is no network and nothing can be installed, so any search you run has to use what is already here (Python 3.13 and its standard library).
