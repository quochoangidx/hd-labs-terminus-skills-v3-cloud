The link team's transmit arbiter, `/app/rtl/vc_arb.v`, is only a stub. Implement the module so it behaves exactly as the timing document `/app/docs/spec.md` describes. The block takes bytes from three virtual channels into two-entry buffers, spends a per-channel credit on every byte it grants, and feeds one output register that the link drains with a valid/ready handshake. The document numbers every rule for one clock edge: which edges are grant opportunities, which channels are eligible, how a channel keeps or loses its run of grants, what a flush discards, and how a returned credit, a loaded credit count and a spent credit combine at the same edge. Keep the port list, names and widths exactly as they are in the stub.

Your module will be compiled with Icarus Verilog (`iverilog -g2005`) against a bench that drives stimulus it generates, and checked on every output port at every clock cycle against the behaviour the document defines. That stimulus deliberately makes rules meet at the same edge: credits returned, loaded and spent together, flushes while a grant is pending or while the channel writes, back-pressure that lifts in the cycle a byte could be granted, full buffers that are also being granted, and resets in the middle of traffic. The stimulus stays within the ports and widths of section 1; `cfg_vc` may be 3, and a cycle may assert any combination of inputs. It must also pass `yosys -p "read_verilog rtl/vc_arb.v; synth -top vc_arb"` with no latch inferred. `sh /app/sim/run_smoke.sh` runs that synthesis and a short directed bench, which you are free to extend; passing it says little about the graded stimulus.

Section 7 of the document holds the build rules and they are enforced on the source: one file, one clock, synthesizable Verilog-2005, no `initial` blocks, no delays, no system tasks or functions such as `$display` or `$fopen`, no `` `include ``, and no hierarchical (dotted) names. Additional modules inside the same file are fine, and so are generate blocks, functions and parameters. Only `/app/rtl/vc_arb.v` is graded; anything you add under `/app/sim/` is ignored.
