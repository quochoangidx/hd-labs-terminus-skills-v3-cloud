`/app/src/costshare` prices a family's claim lines for the benefit year, and it no longer splits a line between the plan and the member the way its design note, `/app/docs/cost-sharing.md`, says it should. The note is the authority. Change the package so that it does what the note says, keeping its public surface as it is: `Plan`, `Line`, `Result`, `share`, `Ledger` with `record` and its four total readers, and `Adjudicator` with `adjudicate`, `deductible_left` and its `ledger` attribute.

Every rule in the note holds for all the values it is written for. Where the note says nothing, what the package does today stands: a helper keeps the result it gives today for an argument or a plan setting the note does not describe, and that result is used wherever the note places the helper. Add no exception, clamp or guard to what a method or helper accepts now.

The package will be driven with plans and line sequences of a few to a few hundred lines across several members: lines that meet the deductible part way, families that reach a family limit before any member reaches their own, lines that land exactly on a maximum, lines priced after a maximum is reached, amounts of zero or less, and settings outside the ranges some of the note's rules are written for. `share` and `Ledger.record` are also called directly. Every `Result` field (`allowed`, `deductible`, `copay`, `coinsurance`, `member_total` and `plan_pays`), every ledger total and every `deductible_left` answer is compared exactly, in cents.
