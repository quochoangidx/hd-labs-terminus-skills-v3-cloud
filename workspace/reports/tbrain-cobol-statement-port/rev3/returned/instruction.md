The quarterly water statements are still printed by the COBOL job step `/app/legacy/WBILL.cbl`, with its copybooks beside it and its run notes in `/app/legacy/RUNBOOK.md`. Write its replacement in `/app/port/wbill.py`: Python 3, standard library only, run as `python3 /app/port/wbill.py <readings> <payments> <output>`, where the three paths stand for the job step's `CUSTIN`, `PAYIN` and `STMTOUT`. For any pair of input files the job step accepts, the port must write exactly the bytes the job step writes to `STMTOUT` when it is built and run as the run notes describe. `/app/legacy/samples` holds a production reading extract and payments export and the statement file the job step produced from them. There is no COBOL compiler in this environment.

The port will be run on files of up to 500 records each: ordinary district runs, and files that go through every path of the program. On the readings side that means rejected records, meter rollover, credit balances, very large commercial accounts, names of every shape, amounts on rounding boundaries, lines shorter than a full record, and a header that is missing or out of place. On the payments side it means every posting kind, amounts written every way the program accepts or refuses, lines with fewer or more fields than usual, unknown and repeated accounts, and memos of any length.
