The advertising desk makes up its pages with `/app/planner/plan_edition.py`, which takes the booked ads and then the rest, biggest earners first, and drops each on the first page with room for it, trying the pages of its own section first, and its layouts leave paying ads out of the paper and put others on pages their advertisers did not ask for. For each edition in `/app/editions/` (`mon`, `wed`, `fri` and `sat`), write a layout to `/app/layouts/<edition>.json` that follows `/app/docs/makeup-rules.md` and costs no more than the edition's target in `/app/editions/targets.json`. Each target is the cost of the best layout the desk has found for that edition.

`/app/tools/check_layout.py` checks a layout under the rules and prints its cost; the rules document gives the file format. Only the four layout files are used, and how you produce them is up to you, but there is no network and nothing can be installed, so any search you run has to use what is already here (Python 3.13 and its standard library).
