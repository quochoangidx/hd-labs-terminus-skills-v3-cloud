# Demurrage Tariff DM-3

North Quay Container Terminal, commercial office. Third issue.

This tariff sets the demurrage charged on import containers left in the
terminal after discharge. Where the billing package and this tariff disagree,
the tariff governs. Rule numbers do not move between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 Days are whole days, numbered; money is a whole number of cents.

1.2 This tariff provides for release files within these limits, and each
limit includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 containers, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to
  11 characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a type is "DRY", "REEFER" or "TANK";
- a discharge day is from 0 to 3,650, and a container is picked up on its
  discharge day or up to 120 days later;
- a daily rate is from 1 to 100,000 cents.

## 2 Dwell and free time

2.1 A container's dwell counts every day it stood in the terminal: its
discharge day, its pickup day and every day between.

2.2 A dry container has 5 free days. A reefer container has 3 free days.

2.3 A container whose dwell is longer than its free days is on demurrage. Its
demurrage days are its dwell less its free days.

## 3 Charges

3.1 Every container has demurrage days and a demurrage charge. The demurrage
charge of a container on demurrage is its daily rate for each of its first 4
demurrage days, and twice its daily rate for each demurrage day after the
fourth.

3.2 A statement's total is the sum of its containers' demurrage charges.
