# Overdue Fines Policy LF-5

Harrow County Libraries, circulation office. Fifth issue.

This policy sets the fines charged on loans returned to a branch. Where the
circulation package and this policy disagree, the policy governs. Rule numbers
do not move between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 Days are whole days, numbered from 0; day 6 and every seventh day after it
(every day whose number leaves 6 when divided by 7) is a Sunday. Money is a
whole number of cents.

1.2 This policy provides for return files within these limits, and each limit
includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 loans, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to 10
  characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a kind is "BOOK", "DVD" or "JOURNAL";
- a borrowing day is from 0 to 3,650, and a loan is returned on its borrowing
  day or up to 180 days later;
- a daily fine is from 1 to 500 cents, and a replacement cost from 100 to
  20,000 cents.

## 2 Due day and late days

2.1 Every loan has a loan period, and its due day is its borrowing day plus
its loan period. The loan period of a book is 21 days. The loan period of a
DVD is 7 days.

2.2 A loan returned after its due day is late. The branches are closed on
Sundays, and a Sunday is never a late day: a late loan's late days are the
days after its due day, up to and including its return day, that are not
Sundays.

## 3 Fines

3.1 Every loan has late days and a fine. The fine of a late loan is its daily
fine for each late day, but never more than its replacement cost.

3.2 A statement's total is the sum of its loans' fines.
