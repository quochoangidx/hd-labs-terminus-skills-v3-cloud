The package in `/app/src/metalquant` reduces an ICP-MS trace-metal batch to the numbers the laboratory reports, and it no longer does so the way the laboratory's procedure, `/app/docs/reduction-sop.md` (SOP TM-07), says it should. The SOP is the authority. Change the package so that the report printed by `python3 /app/tools/metalquant_run.py BATCH.json` follows the SOP.

Leave `/app/tools/metalquant_run.py` exactly as it is: batches are reduced by running our own copy of that driver against your `/app/src`, and the file you leave there must still match it byte for byte.

Every rule in the SOP holds for every batch within the limits of its section 1, not only for batches like the example in `/app/examples/`. Where the SOP gives no rule for a value, keep the calculation the code makes for that value today from the values the SOP does define; every other step still follows the SOP. Add no correction, qualifier, exclusion or guard that the SOP does not give.

Batches of one to eighty runs and one to four analytes will be reduced: batches with no, one or several method-blank results and blanks placed anywhere in the run order, readings below nought, values that land exactly on a detection limit or a limit of quantitation, dilutions from 1 to 1000, CCVs that pass and fail with samples before the first CCV, after the last one or in batches with none, and spikes and parents at every level, including non-detects. Every `id`, `analyte`, `flag`, `ccv_ok` and `pass` in the report and the order of every list are compared exactly, `value` is compared as a number or as `null`, and every other number must agree with the expected one to within one part in a million of its size, or within 10^-6 when the expected number is smaller than one.
