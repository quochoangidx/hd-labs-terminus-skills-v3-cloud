Our billing office has been checking the invoices that the parcel billing package in `/app/src/parcelbill` produces against the depot's billing rules, `/app/docs/parcel-billing-rules.md` (PB-4), and they do not agree. Bulky light boxes are being billed as if they weighed less than the rules say, a parcel of 7.2 pounds is billed as 7 pounds, ground deliveries to homes are short on their surcharge, and the fuel line leaves out part of what it should be taken on and drops fractions of a cent the rules round. The rules are the authority for these invoices. Change the package so that the invoice printed by `python3 /app/tools/parcelbill_run.py MANIFEST.json` follows them. Each rule holds for every manifest within the limits of rule 1.3, not only for manifests like the one in `/app/examples/`. Manifests will be billed by running our own copy of `/app/tools/parcelbill_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave that driver exactly as it is; the file you leave there has to match ours byte for byte.

Only change what the rules govern. Where the rules give no rule for a figure that one of the package's steps works out, that step keeps working the figure out the way it does today, from the figures the rules do define, and every other step still follows the rules. Add no exception, clamp or guard that the rules do not give. Some inputs are left entirely open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them: sides, weights, zones, services, counts or lengths outside the limits of rule 1.3, two parcels with the same id, and files that do not follow the README.

Manifests of one to three hundred parcels will be billed. They include every zone and both services, home and business deliveries, sides from 1 to 108 inches in any order, flat and thin parcels as well as boxes, actual weights from a tenth of a pound to 150 pounds, parcels whose actual and dimensional weights land exactly on a whole pound, on either side of each other and far apart, and fuel surcharges that land exactly on a half cent. Every key the README lists is compared exactly, as a JSON integer or string, and so is the order of the parcels.
