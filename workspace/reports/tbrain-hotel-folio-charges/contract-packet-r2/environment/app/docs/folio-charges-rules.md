# Folio Charges Rules FC-3

Harbourview Hotel, front office accounts. Third issue.

These rules set the charges on a guest's folio at checkout. Where the folio
package and these rules disagree, the rules govern. Rule numbers do not move
between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 Money is a whole number of cents. Every figure is worked out from exact,
unrounded amounts.

1.2 These rules provide for night audit files within these limits, and each
limit includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 stays, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to 10
  characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a stay is from 1 to 60 nights;
- a nightly rate is from 1,000 to 150,000 cents.

## 2 Charges

2.1 A stay's room charge is its nightly rate for each of its nights, except
that every seventh night of the stay (its seventh, fourteenth, twenty-first
night and so on) is free.

2.2 Every stay carries a city tax. The city tax of a stay at a nightly rate of
5,000 cents or more is 250 cents for each of its nights, up to 14 nights; no
city tax is charged for any night after the fourteenth.

2.3 A stay's occupancy tax is 13.5 per cent of its room charge, rounded to
the nearest cent, an exact half cent going up.

2.4 Every folio carries a service fee.

2.5 A folio's total is its room charge, occupancy tax, city tax and service
fee added together. A file's total is the sum of its folio totals.
