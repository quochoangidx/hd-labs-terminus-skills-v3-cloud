Our front office has been checking the folios that the package in `/app/src/folio` produces at checkout against the hotel's folio charges rules, `/app/docs/folio-charges-rules.md` (FC-3), and they do not agree. Long-stay guests are being charged for the free nights the rules give them, the city tax is at the old per-night amount and never stops, and the occupancy tax is at the old rate and does not round the way the rules say. The rules are the authority for these folios. Change the package so that the folios printed by `python3 /app/tools/folio_run.py STAYS.json` follow them. Each rule holds for every night audit file within the limits of rule 1.2, not only for files like the one in `/app/examples/`. Files will be billed by running our own copy of `/app/tools/folio_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave that driver exactly as it is; the file you leave there has to match ours byte for byte.

Only change what the rules govern. Where the rules give no rule for a figure that one of the package's steps works out, that step keeps working the figure out the way it does today, from the figures the rules do define, and every other step still follows the rules. Add no exception, clamp or guard that the rules do not give. Some inputs are left entirely open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them: nights, rates, counts or lengths outside the limits of rule 1.2, two stays with the same id, and files that do not follow the README.

Night audit files of one to two hundred stays will be billed. They include stays of one night, of six, seven and eight nights, of thirteen to fifteen, and up to sixty, nightly rates across their whole range, and room charges whose taxes and fees land exactly on a half cent. Every key the README lists is compared exactly, as a JSON integer or string, and so is the order of the folios.
