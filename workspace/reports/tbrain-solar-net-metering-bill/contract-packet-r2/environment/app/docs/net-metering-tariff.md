# Net Metering Tariff NM-4

Brightwater Energy Cooperative, billing office. Fourth issue.

This tariff sets the charges on the bills of customers whose meters both take
electricity from the grid and send it back. Where the billing package and this
tariff disagree, the tariff governs. Rule numbers do not move between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 Energy is a whole number of kilowatt-hours. Money is a whole number of
cents.

1.2 This tariff provides for meter reads files within these limits, and each
limit includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 meters, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to 10
  characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a tariff is "HOME", "FARM" or "SHOP";
- a meter imported and exported from 0 to 50,000 kilowatt-hours each in the
  cycle.

## 2 Energy

2.1 A meter's net is the kilowatt-hours it imported less those it exported. A
meter whose net is more than nought is a net consumer.

2.2 The energy charge of a net consumer on the HOME tariff is 28 cents for
each of the first 300 kilowatt-hours of its net and 34 cents for each
kilowatt-hour after. The energy charge of a net consumer on the FARM tariff is
22 cents, and on the SHOP tariff 31 cents, for each kilowatt-hour of its net.

## 3 Service charge and total

3.1 The service charge of a HOME meter that imported 200 kilowatt-hours or
more in the cycle is 950 cents, and of such a SHOP meter 1,450 cents.

3.2 A bill's total is its energy charge and service charge added together. A
file's total is the sum of its bill totals.
