Our water-quality lab reduces its five-day BOD bench sheets with the package in `/app/src/bodcalc`, and the data reviewers keep sending batches back: seeded effluent results do not match their hand calculations, bottles the bench would never read are being averaged in, less-than results look too low, the dilution-water qualifier does not follow the blanks, duplicate agreement disagrees with the QC sheet, and results come out to a tenth instead of the figures the lab reports. The lab's procedure, SOP WQ-14 in `/app/docs/sop-wq14-bod5.md`, is the authority; the data layout is in `/app/README.md` and a small batch is in `/app/examples/`. Change the package so that the report printed by `python3 /app/tools/bodcalc_run.py BATCH.json` follows the SOP. Each of its rules holds for every batch within the limits of its section 1, not only for batches like the example. Leave `/app/tools/bodcalc_run.py` as it is: batches will be reduced by running our own copy of that driver against your `/app/src`, and the driver you leave behind must still match it byte for byte.

Change only what the SOP rules on. Where the SOP leaves a figure without a definition for a particular batch, that figure is still reported: the package's present calculation for it stays exactly as it is, applied to the quantities the SOP does define, and every later step that uses the figure follows the SOP as usual. Add no exception, clamp or guard that the SOP does not give. Batches outside the limits of section 1, or that break its structural rules, are left open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them.

Batches of one to forty samples will be reduced, each sample of one to five bottles, with one to six seed controls in any mix of usable and unusable ones, one to four dilution-water blanks and a check of one to three bottles, at least one of them usable. Bottles will be usable, short of the depletion limit, spent, or with a final DO above the initial one; samples will be reported with each of the three relations; bottles of one sample may hold the same volume; and duplicates may name a sample listed before or after them, whatever that sample's relation. Sample volumes run from 0.5 to 300 mL, check volumes from 2 to 12 mL, seed volumes from 0 to 3 mL in sample and check bottles and from 5 to 30 mL in seed controls, DO readings from 0.00 to 9.50 mg/L, blank depletions from -0.50 to 1.00 mg/L and seed-control depletions up to 0.3 mg/L per mL of seed, with values close to every limit of the SOP on both sides, as close as its section 1.5 allows. In the report, `batch`, `qualifiers`, every `id`, `relation`, `of` and `pass` and the order of every list are compared exactly; every other number must be a floating-point number agreeing with the expected one to within one part in a million, or within 10^-6 when the expected number is smaller than one.
