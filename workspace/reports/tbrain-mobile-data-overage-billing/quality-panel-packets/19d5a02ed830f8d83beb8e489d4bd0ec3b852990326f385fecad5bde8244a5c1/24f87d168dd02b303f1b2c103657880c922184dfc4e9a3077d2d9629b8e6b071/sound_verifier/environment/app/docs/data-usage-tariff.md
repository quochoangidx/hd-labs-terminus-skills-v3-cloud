# Data Usage Tariff MD-2

Fjordline Mobile, business billing. Second issue.

This tariff sets how a business line's data use in one billing cycle is
measured and charged. Where the billing package and this tariff disagree, the
tariff governs. Rule numbers do not move between issues.

## 1 Units and limits

1.1 A megabyte is 1,024 kilobytes. Usage and allowances are whole megabytes;
money is a whole number of cents.

1.2 This tariff provides for account files within these limits, and each limit
includes its ends:

- a file lists from 1 to 200 lines, and no two share an id; an id is 1 to 10
  characters, each a capital letter or a digit;
- a plan is "BASIC", "PLUS" or "FLEX";
- a line has from 0 to 60 sessions, and a session used from 1 to 2,000,000
  kilobytes;
- an overage rate is from 1 to 500 cents per megabyte.

## 2 Usage and allowance

2.1 Each session is measured on its own in whole megabytes, a part of a
megabyte counting as a whole one. A line's usage is the sum of its sessions'
megabytes.

2.2 Every line has an allowance. The allowance of a BASIC line is 2,048
megabytes. The allowance of a PLUS line is 10,240 megabytes.

2.3 A line whose usage is more than its allowance is over its allowance. Its
overage is its usage less its allowance.

## 3 Charges

3.1 Every line has an overage and a charge. The charge of a line over its
allowance is its overage rate for each of its first 1,024 megabytes of
overage, and 1 cent for each megabyte of overage after those.

3.2 A statement's total is the sum of its lines' charges.
