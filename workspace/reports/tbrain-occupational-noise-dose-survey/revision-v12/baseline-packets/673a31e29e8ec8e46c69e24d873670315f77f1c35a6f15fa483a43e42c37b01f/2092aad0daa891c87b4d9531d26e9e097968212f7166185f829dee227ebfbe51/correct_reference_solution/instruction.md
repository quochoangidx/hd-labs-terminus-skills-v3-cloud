Our hearing conservation lead has been checking the exposure reports that the survey package in `/app/src/noisedose` produces against hand calculations from the programme's noise survey manual, `/app/docs/noise-survey-manual.md` (HC-4), and they do not agree. Doses and TWAs are off across the board, people on ten- and twelve-hour shifts whose dosimeters ran most of the day come out lower than their logs support, the press-shop group sheet showed the group short of the limit while most of its members were over it, and a riveter whose dosimeter held a 140.0 dBC peak was never flagged for impulse noise. The manual is the authority for this compliance report. Change the package so that the report printed by `python3 /app/tools/noisedose_run.py SURVEY.json` follows it. Each of its rules holds for every survey within the limits of its section 1, not only for surveys like the one in `/app/examples/`. Surveys will be reduced by running our own copy of `/app/tools/noisedose_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave that driver exactly as it is; the file you leave there has to match ours byte for byte.

Only change what the manual governs. Where the manual gives no rule for a figure that one of the package's steps works out, keep the figure that step works out today, from the figures the manual does define; every other step still follows the manual. Add no exception, clamp or guard that the manual does not give. Some inputs are left entirely open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them: levels, peaks, lengths or counts outside the limits of section 1, a log whose runs add up to more than 2,880 minutes, two workers with the same id, and files that do not follow the README.

Surveys of one to three hundred workers under one to forty group codes will be reduced. They include shifts from one hour to a full day, logs from no runs at all up to 1,500 runs, runs at every level section 1 allows, readings exactly at the threshold level and the ceiling level and up to 140.0 dBA, TWAs that land exactly on the action level or the limit once rounded, surveys that sample anything from a few minutes of the shift to more than all of it, quiet workers whose readings never reach the threshold, groups of one worker and of many, and peaks exactly at 140.0 dBC. The `survey`, `id`, `group`, `status`, `ceiling` and `impulse` values and the order of both lists are compared exactly, and so is `twa`, which has to be the same tenth of a decibel or `null` where the expected one is. Each `dose` is the unrounded figure: one within one part in 10^11 of the expected dose's size (within 10^-11 when the expected dose is below one) passes, and one further off than one part in 10^10 (10^-10 below one) fails.
