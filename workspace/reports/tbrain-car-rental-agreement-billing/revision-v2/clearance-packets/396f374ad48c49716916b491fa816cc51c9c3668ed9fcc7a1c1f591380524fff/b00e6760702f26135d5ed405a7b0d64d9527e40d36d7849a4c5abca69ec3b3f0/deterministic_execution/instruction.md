Our billing office has been checking the bills that the charging package in `/app/src/rentcharge` produces for closed rental agreements against the branch's charges manual, `/app/docs/rental-charges-manual.md` (RC-3), and they do not agree. A customer who brought a car back twenty minutes after the hour it went out three days earlier was billed a fourth day, weekly renters are being charged for miles the manual lets them drive free, a van whose odometer turned over came out with thousands of negative miles, cars brought back short of fuel are missing the refuelling fee, and the tax line is taxing fuel and dropping fractions of a cent it should round. The manual is the authority for these bills. Change the package so that the bill run printed by `python3 /app/tools/rentcharge_run.py AGREEMENTS.json` follows it. Each of its rules holds for every agreement file within the limits of its rule 1.3, not only for files like the one in `/app/examples/`. Agreement files will be billed by running our own copy of `/app/tools/rentcharge_run.py` against your `/app/src`, so leave that driver exactly as it is; the file you leave there has to match ours byte for byte.

Only change what the manual governs. Where the manual gives no rule for a figure that one of the package's steps works out, that step keeps working the figure out the way it does today, from the figures the manual does define, and every other step still follows the manual. Add no exception, clamp or guard that the manual does not give. Some inputs are left entirely open, and the package may do whatever it likes with them: times, readings, fuel levels, rates, counts or lengths outside the limits of rule 1.3, two agreements with the same id, and files that do not follow the README.

Agreement files of one to two hundred agreements will be billed. They include rentals from one minute to sixty days long, rentals that come back on the minute they went out on a later day and up to and past an hour after it, rentals across month, year and leap-day ends, odometers that turn over, cars that come back with any fuel level from empty to full, day, mile and fuel rates across their whole ranges including rates of nought, and taxes that land exactly on a half cent. Every key the README lists is compared exactly, the branch and each id as the string given and every other figure as a JSON integer, and so is the order of the bills.
