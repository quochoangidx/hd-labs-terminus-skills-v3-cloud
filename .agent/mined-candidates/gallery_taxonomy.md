## Data model — the gallery MERGES two sources

`/portal/tasks` concatenates:
1. **`v_tasks_with_priorities`** — `category` → `subcategory` → `subsubcategory`
   (3-level tree below) + `difficulty` (easy|medium|hard, UI = 1/2/3 dots).
2. **`task_inspiration_v2`** — `type` (= category) + `subtypes` (the 5 cross-cutting
   axes) + `languages`, mapped into the card as `category=type`,
   `subcategory=subtypes.join(', ')`.

Two orthogonal axes therefore exist; record BOTH where they apply: the **3-level
category taxonomy** AND zero-or-more of the **5 subtypes**.
